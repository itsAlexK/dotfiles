# Amazon Writing Layer

Use these principles for clarity and persuasion in addition to the measured [prose profile](prose-profile.md). The prose profile wins where generic guidance conflicts with the user's observed voice.

## Reader and purpose

- Identify the decision, reader, prior knowledge, and requested action before writing.
- Put the main point and recommendation near the start. Do not make a busy reader reconstruct the ask.
- Include context required by an informed reader outside the immediate team. Remove team history that does not change the decision.
- Apply the "so what" test to every section, table, and appendix. If removing it would not affect the decision or implementation, remove or link it.

## Concrete claims

- Replace evaluative adjectives with data, a comparison, or a named mechanism.
- Separate facts from assumptions and forecasts.
- Prefer active constructions with a named system component or owner.
- Keep the subject and action visible. Do not bury the action under nominalizations or long setup clauses.
- Use examples to discharge abstractions, but keep the example tied to the decision.

## Terms and acronyms

- Define an unfamiliar term at first use before another idea depends on it.
- Expand an unfamiliar acronym at first use and use one stable name afterward.
- Avoid an acronym used only a few times.
- Use a glossary as an index, not as a substitute for first-use definitions.

## Detail and appendices

Keep the reasoning needed for the decision in the body. Put raw benchmark output, exhaustive schemas, configuration listings, long requirement inventories, and exploratory dead ends in appendices or linked artifacts.

An appendix cannot repair an unsupported body claim. Summarize the decisive evidence locally and link the full result.

## Editing

- Remove repeated context, throat-clearing, empty transitions, and claims that only restate headings.
- Prefer ordinary words over internal shorthand when both are precise.
- Keep diagrams labeled and explain the decision-relevant point in adjacent text.
- Preserve calibrated uncertainty. Remove vague hedging, but retain explicit forecasts, external dependencies, confidence limits, and unresolved facts.
- Do not force every sentence to be short. Apply the measured sentence and paragraph distribution in the prose profile.
