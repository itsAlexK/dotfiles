# Press Release/Frequently Asked Questions (PRFAQ) / Working Backwards

## Purpose

A PRFAQ asks whether a clearly identified customer problem, differentiated future experience, and viable business opportunity justify investment. It decides what should be built and why before an HLD decides how.

Start before requesting substantial budget, assigning an implementation team, or selecting architecture. Rejection is a valid result when the customer benefit or viability does not survive the difficult questions.

## Reader contract

The press release makes the future customer outcome concrete in approximately one page. The FAQ then discloses customer questions and internal feasibility, economics, risk, and evidence.

Do not add a separate executive summary by default. The press release is the concise narrative. Preserve a destination template when it requires a different form.

## Progressive section routing

Use this overview to decide fit and shape. Read [prfaq-sections.md](prfaq-sections.md) when drafting or reviewing the press release and FAQs.

The press release and FAQ split defines the document type, but individual questions and internal subsections remain demand-driven. Each section reference includes a principle and a synthetic example for when that question applies. The example shows how to advance the customer or investment argument and is not customer evidence.

## Core questions

Before drafting, answer:

1. Who is the customer?
2. What problem or opportunity do they have?
3. What is the most important customer benefit?
4. How do we know what customers need?
5. What does the future experience look like?
6. Why is the outcome meaningfully better than alternatives?
7. What must be true for the business and operation to work?

Keep customer evidence, scenario assumptions, and forecasts separate.

## Boundaries

A PRFAQ should include enough technical feasibility to expose a fatal dependency or cost, but it should not contain a preferred architecture unless the customer promise depends on that mechanism.

Use:

- A PRFAQ for customer value and viability.
- A one-pager for a narrow technical direction.
- An HLD for system architecture.
- An LLD for implementation behavior.

## Review process

Review repeatedly while the customer promise and scope remain cheap to change. Seek product, engineering, finance, legal, security, operations, support, and sales input where those functions determine viability.

The review should attack the hardest assumptions. A long FAQ that avoids pricing, adoption, operational burden, privacy, or resource needs is not thorough.

## PRFAQ review checks

- The customer and painful problem are specific.
- The headline and first paragraph lead with an outcome, not a feature list.
- Evidence distinguishes observed need from a forecast.
- The future experience is concrete enough to reveal missing requirements.
- External FAQs answer adoption, availability, support, security, and alternatives.
- Internal FAQs address economics, resources, dependencies, operations, legal risk, metrics, and the minimum viable scope.
- Difficult questions receive direct answers or are marked unresolved.
- Architecture detail appears only when it determines customer value or feasibility.

## Example dissections

A strong press release connects a named customer problem to an observable future interaction: a compact narrative with an immediate benefit and a visualized customer experience. A strong FAQ shows iteration, audience segmentation, difficult questions answered directly, and experience mocks. The FAQ is where a plausible press release is tested against delivery and business reality.

Authentic pre-launch PRFAQs are rarely public. Public practitioner templates should be labeled as guidance, not represented as original product documents.
